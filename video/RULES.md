# Video rules: Builder.io Agent-Native Hackathon (Oct 27 – Nov 9 2026)

Read 2026-11-09. R = https://www.builder.io/hackathon/rules · H = https://www.builder.io/hackathon ·
S = https://www.builder.io/hackathon/submit · C = https://www.agent-native.com/docs/template-clips-features/

| # | Rule (verbatim) | Source | Class | What the film does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Record a public Clips demonstration showing the actual application in use." | R, How to Enter 4 | **Hard** (required field, entry incomplete without it) | The film is uploaded to Clips ("Upload a video file", C) and every product shot is a real capture of the deployed app. |
| 2 | "The recording must be viewable without a login or access request." | R | **Hard** | Clip set to "Anyone with the link", no password, no expiry; checked from a logged-out browser. |
| 3 | "Plagiarism, fabricated demonstrations, fraud ... may result in disqualification." | R | **Hard** | Real UI only, real numbers read from files at render time, personas and scripted inputs labelled on screen. |
| 4 | "Entries must be truthful, lawful, and submitted with the necessary permissions." | R | **Hard** | Only CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA footage and composed music; every outside asset in `LICENSES.md`. |
| 5 | "Identify pre-existing work, templates, libraries, third-party assets, and what changed during the event." | R, How to Enter 2 | Hard for the form, not the film | Goes in the submission description (the user writes it). The film never names the base repo. |
| 6 | "Do not upload tax forms, identity documents, or payment credentials to the public demo" | R | **Hard** | Nothing private on screen: no keys, cookies, emails, chat ids. |
| 7 | Length | R, H, S | **None stated** (no maxlength anywhere) | Soft target ~2:30 (user's choice), may run to ~2:45 if the film is clearly better; everything essential front-loaded. |
| 8 | Judging: "Usefulness and Execution: 40%. A clear problem and a complete working flow." · "Creativity and Design: 20%" · "Innovation: 20%. A thoughtful people-and-agent workflow" · "Cleverness and Use of Agent-Native: 20%. Resourceful use of Agent-Native to bring the interface, agent behavior, and application capabilities together." | R | Rubric | One complete hero flow (two devices) early; the agent visibly doing the work; one action set named in the arch3d section; measured proof. |
| 9 | Voice / TTS / music / speed-ups | R, H, S | **Not mentioned** | Narrator (local TTS) allowed; any speed-up of a real take is labelled "sped up ×N" on screen. |
| 10 | Platform | R | Clips (public link); YouTube optional | Master 1080p for Clips + YouTube; 720p phone copy. |

Ambiguity: rule 1 says "Clips demonstration" and Clips accepts uploaded video files (C: "Upload a video file. Add an existing
video straight from your computer"). Stricter reading applied: the film's product shots are all real captures of the live app,
so the upload is a demonstration of the actual application in use.
